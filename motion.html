How It Works

1. Rigid Ribcage & Conforming Cartilages

Ribs 1–10 rotate rigidly about their posterior costovertebral axes with chord-length scaling for short upper ribs, while a tetrahedral cartilage network maintains sub-millimeter bone–cartilage attachment.

2. Coupled Viscera & Abdominal Mechanics

Internal organs slide smoothly inside the thoracic and peritoneal cavities, while incompressible abdominal coupling (Konno–Mead mechanics) translates diaphragm descent into anterior abdominal wall expansion.

3. Invertible 4D Meshes & Volumes

Stationary velocity integration produces fold-free, invertible deformation fields (min Jacobian > 0) that simultaneously deform surface meshes, internal vasculature, and 4D attenuation volumes.

Broader context: Moving from static anatomical twins to controllable, physiologically grounded 4D human models establishes a foundation for virtual patient populations, imaging physics simulation, and broader biomedical modeling and in-silico testing.
